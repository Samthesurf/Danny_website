Why DACTION

Small team. Real outcomes. No fluff.

Youth-led, start to finish

Every campaign is designed and run by young people, not handed down by adults on their behalf. DACTION's team plans, fundraises, delivers, and answers for the results.

Built with the community

We work directly with the schools, waste collectors, and coastal communities our programs touch, so the people affected help shape what we do.

Engineering discipline, applied

Led by a First Class engineering graduate, DACTION brings a careful, evidence-first mindset to grassroots environmental work, connecting technical careers with community climate action.

Latest

A founding partnership at Daniel's own university.

DACTION recently became a founding funding partner of ABUADMUN, a new Model United Nations association at Afe Babalola University, backing it with a multi-year funding commitment as it holds its first conference in November 2026.
